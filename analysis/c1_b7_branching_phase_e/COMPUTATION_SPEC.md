# Frozen mathematical domains and reference scope

## Dependency specification

The arithmetic input is the pinned Phase-D first-relay gate A7 and exact primitive
factor definitions. E2/E3 identify terminal supports by inclusion of all maximal
generators. E5 selects the 15 first-relay pairs and eight indices per pair.
No generic nonregular-prime scan is a task objective.

## Executed finite domains

1. Replay four complete first-extension cyclotomic products, n=7,14,21,42.
2. Emit C3's 48 source indices and C4_fork's 120 indices, testing cardinality and
   disjointness. Generate serial indices only for five explicitly certified examples.
3. Certify the 13 listed actual regular primes: complete odd trial division,
   exact-order drop tests, and modular square lifting. Diamond 6717031 also has
   a complete-(p-1) Lucas certificate at base 3.
4. Generate every abstract sorted support DAG with total vertex count m=2,...,7,
   last vertex a formal root. Vertex 0 is 7; each other regular vertex has a
   nonempty subset of earlier vertices as admitted support. For every nonempty
   root support compare direct descendant union against mandatory-maxima inclusion.
   Abstract nodes do not claim actual prime values or nonregularity.
5. Replay the actual regular fork mask {7,43,127}, K=2,E={1}, anchor 1,
   d=4 mod6, full L=229362. Compare direct local valuation acceptance and exact
   regular-shell formulas for all 38227 branch exponents and all three rows.
6. Check three exact critical-integer identities for R={7}, {7,43}, {7,43,127}:
   product of all terminal cyclotomic values against Phi_a(5^b), using a separate
   divisor-recursion evaluation. Remove the specified imprimitive factor and
   verify absence of all primes <=max R (49 small-prime divisibility checks total).
   Large integers are recorded by bit length and SHA256 of unsigned big-endian
   bytes; no full factorization is claimed.
7. Run 14 negative regression controls.

All decisions use integers, finite sets, or bitmasks. No floating-point probability
or probable-prime declaration is used as a final certificate.

## Discovery-only progression probe

A small factor probe was used only for two theorem-selected fork indices
n=5461=43*127 and n=10922. It tested q=1+k*n, 1<=k<=40000, q=3 mod4:
10000 and 20000 modular divisibility tests respectively. A hit is separately
certified for primality, exact order, and lifting. It found 6717031 at n=5461,
k=1230. This is an index-first trial-factor discovery step, not a blind scan
for arbitrary nonregular primes. No absence conclusion is promoted. The sealed
reference replay accepts this integer as a candidate and proves its properties;
it does not rerun or depend on the search domain's exhaustiveness.

## Deliberately unexecuted work

No complete factorization of all 48 Task-1 integers; no complete S_r inventory;
no square-hit closure of all 120 fork integers; no enumeration at unbounded M;
no repository-native tests, CI, or historical 2e9 prime scan; no actual nonregular
root above the demonstrated regular states. Finite formulas are not reported as
finished factorizations.
