# Integrality and symmetry audit

All state arithmetic is exact. X=2q is an integer vector with all entries even
or all entries odd. It has squared length 4M. The norm-5 parameter vectors are
also doubled; the denominator for the output X' is 20, not 5 or 10.

The producer first checks divisibility by 20 of C X, then divides, then checks
common parity and norm. For target marking it requires all-even X'. This
prevents a half-integral Hurwitz point from being treated as two Gaussian
integers in Z[i]. The independent replay uses the scalar/vector quaternion
product rather than the producer's 4-by-4 matrices.

For a norm-one unit with doubled vector U2, left multiplication has formula
X'=(U2 X)/2, and right multiplication is analogous. The numerator is even
for every allowed H point. Its output can change the integer/half-integer
coset; no coset is silently discarded while building the H graph.

The finite symmetry group G is generated by all signed coordinate permutations
and by left and right multiplication by every Hurwitz unit. This is finite:
it is a subgroup of the orthogonal automorphism group of the positive-definite
lattice H. Signed permutations, units, and their inverses preserve H. Quaternion
conjugation is already among the signed permutations.

The entire norm-5 operator family is normalized by G. For unit multiplication
this follows from closure of H and its units under multiplication and
conjugation. For signed permutations it is checked, exactly, on the four
single-coordinate sign changes and the three adjacent coordinate swaps;
these generate all signed permutations. This verification is on all 10,368
operator matrices, independent of M. Alternatively it can be checked on the
144-parameter factor list. Thus quotient edges do not depend on a selected
representative of a G orbit.

A graph target is a G orbit containing an actual integral state with a pair
norm 5^d. This is legitimate because the indicated symmetries are explicitly
allowed moves and any pair can be moved into the second pair. A half-integral
state is never itself marked as target. Every G orbit has an integral member,
by the direct recovery proof in QUATERNION_REFORMULATION.md.

The reported graph connectivity is connectivity of this stated symmetry
quotient, equivalently of the graph augmented by these symmetry moves. It is
not a claim about the graph on individual points with the symmetries removed.
Negative obstructions proved in the larger graph automatically apply to the
smaller one; positive reachability is only asserted with the listed symmetries.
