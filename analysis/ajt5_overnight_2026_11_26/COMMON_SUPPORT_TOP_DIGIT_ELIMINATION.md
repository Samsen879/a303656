# Eliminate the highest 7-adic label on common word support

Let x have order 343 in F_p*, let (L,7)=1, and put Z_i=(3^{u_i}5^{v_i})^L. Work on common capture support, where all Z_i have 7-power order. For coarse (e,k), E=e+49j and ℓ_i=u_i+kv_i, write D_i=Z_i x^(−Leℓ_i), T=x^(49L). A shared high digit is possible precisely when D_i=1 wherever 7 divides ℓ_i, all other D_i have seventh power 1, and D_i^ℓ_h=D_h^ℓ_i against a single reference h with ℓ_h prime to 7. If there is no reference then the unit-coefficient conditions are vacuous. This is the consistency test in the cyclic subgroup generated by T.

For two words of determinant Δ=u_1 v_2−u_2 v_1 prime to 7, define Y=Z_1^{v_2} Z_2^{−v_1} and X=Z_1^{−u_2} Z_2^{u_1}. If e is prime to 7 in this chart, the exact condition becomes Y^7=x^(7LeΔ) and X=Y^k. The first equation forces ord(Y)=343 and that the exponent of Y relative to x^(LΔ) equals e modulo 49. The second forces consistency with k. Inverting the two-by-two word matrix on the finite 7-primary subgroup recovers the two original conditions. In particular, distinct k modulo 343 pass on disjoint prime support. Neither test enumerates the seven high digits or assumes two separately captured supports agree.

The actual source (5,296) has coarse menu {(15,10),(15,157),(38,67)}. The preselected pair (1,2), (−1,2) has determinant 4 and common support p=31914396182372383. Its native logs are (90,169); their actual ratio 181 modulo 343 excludes all three tags. The remaining prime q is 1 modulo 1372 and outside the inert sign branch. The exact replay reconstructs the menu, the common gcd, the prime certificates and both native logs. It also gives two same-p short words that keep complementary subsets, plus a linearly independent pair of determinant 7^5 that keeps **every** high digit. The full blind-word lattice in this fixture is 49|v and 343|(u+v): comparing all high digits and the two k classes first forces 7|u,v; writing u=7a,v=7b gives the equations checked at the end of the script. This is local chart evidence, not a representation for arbitrary n.
