# A true norm pair with exhaustive angular failure

This fixed K=300 target uses actual labels A=9³, B=25³ and a first true primitive critical chord. The second endpoints S2,R2 both have full integer Gaussian norm representations. Cross gcds with the first two endpoints have norms 26 and 2426, with explicitly shared Gaussian factors (1+i)(3+2i) and (1+i)(27+22i) allocated to the second lower and upper endpoint respectively. Yet no choice of representation at either end, even allowing different allocations, can enter the critical window.

For the fixed target the factorization is S2=4·13·p and R2=2·1213·q, with p and q prime and 1 mod 4. The replay checks recursive Pocklington certificates, the Gaussian prime factors, and exact norm identities. Unique factorization in Z[i] implies all S2 coordinates are generated by the four units, the fixed norm-4 ramified factor and independent conjugation of the factors above 13 and p. The analogous list applies to R2, using the fixed norm-2 ramified factor and the primes above 1213 and q. Each list has 16 elements. Enumerating all 256 pairs yields 32 squared-chord halves q_chord=|P−Q|²/2 and their minimum is more than 7600 times S2/(64 K²). As the chart requires q_chord<S2/(64 K²), the angle fails before any additional primitivity restriction is applied.

The earlier K=692 positive and inert-negative fixtures show that a gcd pattern can coexist with true second angles or with outright norm failure. This exact control shows a third possibility: both norms and allocated cross factors exist, but the angle is impossible at this target. It does not assert an asymptotic classification.
