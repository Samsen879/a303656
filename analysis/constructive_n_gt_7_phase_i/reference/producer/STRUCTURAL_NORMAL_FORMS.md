# N=8 STRUCTURAL NORMAL FORMS / N=9 STRUCTURAL NORMAL FORMS

## What is classified, and what is not

This file gives exhaustive necessary **terminal-core counts**, possible first nonlinear ranks/arities under the imported sparse model, all rigid-only ternary leaf-count shapes at sizes 7 and9, and two explicit conditional complete architectures. It does not claim a complete arithmetic classification of every N=8/N=9 order DAG, shared-state pattern, precision choice, or nonlinear alternative. That broader classification remains unproved. A formal slot is not an actual prime.

For N<=12, let H={q:T(q) subset {3,5}}. The source theorem requires |H|>=7, with pure-3 equality case. At p>N elimination is necessarily linear, not optional. Extra origins can be inactive, redundant, useful only on another parity, useful in a different nonlinear witness, or useful because they split a coarse duty. They are not automatically slack capacity in the same witness.

## N=8: exhaustive terminal-core count cases

1. **8A:** |H|=7, |Q\H|=1. The seven H roots are pure-3; their distinguished surviving core retains the seven-source normal form, including its seven-leaf frontier. The free-terminal extra is removed before the small nonlinear core. It does not repair O31. This is not a constructive escape.
2. **8B:** |H|=8. Source equality does not apply. This can include all eight pure-3 roots or at least one {3,5}/{5}-terminal root. It does not imply any of these numerical prime inventories exists. Nonregular rows may now have different parity roles, and the unused eighth rigid-origin slot on one parity can be useful on the other.

Before the first essential nonlinear event there are at most eight sources. Allowed first-coordinate/irredundant-origin-count pairs from p<=r and r=1 mod(p-1) are

    p=3: r=3,5,7; p=5: r=5; p=7: r=7.

For a COMPLETE state whose first nonlinear coordinate is 3, the imported shallow-head restrictions rule out the smaller final frontiers, leaving a seven-leaf (2,2,3) frontier. This is a statement about one boundary witness, not a proof that N=8 globally has the exactly-seven disjointness/O31 architecture. An eighth original nonregular row can play a helper role or an opposite-parity role, so the equality proof cannot simply be reused.

For first rank5 or7, this package does not supply an exhaustive residual/witness classification or a theorem of impossibility. In particular, counting only one chosen nonlinear alternative would be unsound.

### Explicit conditional 8B-P architecture

THEOREMS I6 has eight pure-3 terminal slots. Seven origins survive to the selected even-parity frontier; the eighth lineage is lost there at its parity-inactive regular relay, but supplies a separate odd-parity 31 duty. Its original root has odd order and can still have incidental fatal points on the even parity; it is the full-basin residual that vanishes. Its two 31 modules share only the actual regular row31, with identical r31=4, and each contains an even-order regular relay. Thus no all-odd31 basin is a required hypothesis of this sufficient architecture.

Prime-terminal existence remains open. The positive conclusion is a consistent conditional full-state construction, not an actual counterexample to a universal O31 conjecture.

## N=9: exhaustive terminal-core count cases

1. **9A:** |H|=7 and two large-terminal extras. Equality normal form persists; the extras cannot repair it in the small core.
2. **9B:** |H|=8 and one large-terminal extra. Only the eight-origin core can supply the small nonlinear construction; the ninth row is not automatically useful there.
3. **9C:** |H|=9. Pure and mixed {3,5} signatures are not separated by the equality theorem. Arithmetic and state feasibility still need proof.

Allowed first-coordinate/irredundant-origin-count pairs are

    p=3: r=3,5,7,9; p=5: r=5,9; p=7: r=7.

For a complete first-rank3 boundary, the minimal full frontier has 7 or9 leaves. A rigid-only full ternary tree with nine leaves has four internal vertices. Its depth-count profile must be one of

    (0,9), (1,5,3), (2,1,6), (2,2,2,3).

Derivation: distribute the three internal vertices below the root among its three child subtrees. An internal path of length4 gives (2,2,2,3); two internal grandchildren in the same first subtree give (2,1,6); two internal first subtrees with a further split give (1,5,3); three internal first subtrees give (0,9).

The shallow actual-head inventory excludes (0,9) for a complete linearly descended pure-3 frontier: one first branch is outside the two fixed first shadows of7 and31; its three second-level leaves would require three different depth-two heads, but only19 and5167 exist. A shallower head's fixed first cylinder cannot lie in that missing branch, and raw nonregular pure order9/18 resources do not exist. The other three profiles are candidate shapes, not certified prime realizations.

For a rigid-only full5-frontier, the analogous count profiles at5 or9 leaves are (5) or(4,5). Dynamic-center witnesses can require a more careful relative-depth interpretation; no exhaustive p5/p7 mixed-DAG normal form is asserted here.

### Explicit conditional 9C-S architecture

THEOREMS I7 realizes profile (1,5,3) with nine uninstantiated nonregular slots and actual regular skeleton. Three order279 roots share31, serving 0,3,6 mod9. This is not the old seven-origin normal form with an unused extra row. The three origins are simultaneously required to complete the formerly single first-level duty.

The final providers of those three depth-two leaves are the distinct roots, since their own order guards contain 3^2. Row31's first-level shadow is shared as a consistent helper, not falsely charged as three independent original providers. The source capacity semantics are therefore respected.

## Strategic conclusion

The smallest structurally plausible N>7 in this session is8. It is not proved minimal among actual complete certificates, and no actual N=8 certificate was found. N=9 is the better frozen arithmetic benchmark: its new replacement target Phi279(5) has126 digits, whereas the particular N8 odd-parity chain requires an impractically large prime-index cyclotomic integer. N=10–12 allow more such trades but do not cure a shortage of eligible actual roots. At N13 a new free-terminal threshold becomes possible, not automatically useful.

There is no proof here that N>7 is globally impossible (Level D), nor that a larger N is probabilistically easier. What becomes demonstrably easier is the conditional structural/state interface: the single all-odd31 depth-one endpoint is no longer mandatory as an input to a sufficient construction. Literal-base5 nonregularity still has to be supplied.
