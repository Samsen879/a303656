# Evidence classification

## PROVED

- the order-dependency graph is strictly ascending and acyclic;
- the prime-coordinate strict-deficit criterion;
- the necessary saturated odd-coordinate condition;
- the beta-one aligned-shell saturation and lower-coordinate contraction;
- the common-residue minimum-valuation compatibility rule;
- the exact two-adic anchor count lemma.

The lifting, fatal normal form, and reverse CRT statements used by the proof
were already established on main and are not reclassified as new.

## EXACT FINITE COMPUTATION

- independent full-period enumeration of the `11->67->20771` chain;
- the `670+66+1` saturated fiber and diagnostic hash;
- exact rational examples proving coordinate/global union-bound
  incomparability in both directions;
- the common-`r` dual-anchor sample;
- the 3,960-case narrow active-cover relaxation;
- direct square-residue verification through `K_2=10`.

## OBSERVATION

Actual prime pairs realize the exact `(p-1)/p+1/p=1` beta-one saturation
boundary.  This identifies a recursion mechanism but does not classify it.

## HEURISTIC

No heuristic is used in the accepted theorem statements.

## UNKNOWN

- universal no-go for arbitrary finite entangled systems;
- existence of a complete `C=1` fail-closed local certificate;
- whether recursive contractions can reach a root cover on both anchors;
- any implication from local escape to actual two-square representability.
