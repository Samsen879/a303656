## Audited regular-lift no-go theorem

Let `C>=1`. Let `P` be any finite set of primes `p=3 mod4`, `p!=5`. For each
`p`, fix:

- a precision `K_p>=2`;
- a residue `r_p mod p^K_p` for `n`;
- any set `E_p` of accepted positive odd valuations below `K_p`.

Optionally fix one sound two-adic local obstruction at precision `2^K_2`,
`K_2>=2`. Put

```text
w_p = ord_p(5).
```

Assume every odd prime is base-5 regular-lift:

```text
v_p(5^w_p - 1) = 1,
```

equivalently `ord_(p^k)(5)=w_p p^(k-1)` for every required precision.

Then there exist `c in {0,1}` and `d>=0` such that:

- the two-adic remainder, if present, is locally a sum of two squares; and
- at every `p in P`, the local valuation of
  `r_p-3^c-5^d` is zero, positive even, or unresolved at least `K_p`.

Therefore no certificate in this finite regular-lift local-mask class covers
even the two anchors `c=0,1`. Consequently it cannot cover any strip `C>=1`.

### Proof audit

For the optional two-adic row, select `c in {0,1}` and `m in {0,1}` by the
four residues of `r_2 mod4`, so that `r_2-3^c-m=1 mod4`. Since `5` generates
the subgroup `1+4Z` modulo `2^K_2`, one exponent class makes the local
remainder equal to `m`, hence locally two-square.

Fix a regular odd prime and write `t=r_p-3^c`. If `t modp` is not in the
subgroup generated by `5`, every exponent has valuation zero. Otherwise fix
the coarse logarithm modulo `w_p`. Regular lifting says the subgroup generated
by `5` modulo `p^K_p` is the complete inverse image of the subgroup modulo
`p`; therefore the active coarse class has a unique lifted logarithm `b`
modulo `w_p p^(K_p-1)`. For `d=b+w_p q`, LTE gives, below the precision,

```text
v_p(t-5^d) = 1 + v_p(q).
```

Choosing `q=p mod p^2` makes the valuation `2`, or makes the residue zero at
precision `p^2`, which the verifier correctly treats as unresolved rather
than an exact odd valuation.

To satisfy all primes simultaneously, include the initial two-adic exponent
class, every `w_p`, and every `p^2` in one exponent modulus. Process its
prime-power components in increasing prime order. All prime factors of `w_p`
divide `p-1` and have already been fixed when the `p` component is chosen.
The active/inactive coarse class is therefore known; choose the new `p^2`
component to force the safe lift above. Later components do not change earlier
ones. CRT produces one nonnegative exponent `d` escaping every listed mask.

Independent exact checks confirmed the regular subgroup identity and LTE
geometry for `p=3,7,11,19,31,43`. A complete small model tested all `10,584`
tuples of residues for a `2^3`, `3^3`, `7^2` system with period `126`; every
tuple left at least `66` of its `252` cells uncovered.
